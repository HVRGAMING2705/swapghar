Questions, answered

How do points actually work?

You earn points every night a guest stays at your home, and spend them to stay at any member's home — no need to swap at the same time, or even with the same person. As a guide, 1 point is worth about ₹10 of stay value, and points never expire while your membership is active.

What is a simultaneous swap?

You and another member stay in each other's homes over the same dates. No points change hands — you simply trade stays. It is the classic home exchange, and it costs nothing beyond your membership.

Is my home protected during a swap?

Yes. Every confirmed exchange is covered by SwapGhar home protection up to ₹10 lakh for accidental damage, and only ID-verified members can host or travel. House rules are agreed before the swap begins.

How are members verified?

Every member verifies a government ID and a phone number before they can list a home or request a stay. Reviews can only be left after a completed stay by a real exchange partner — no bought ratings.

Do I need SwapGhar Plus to swap homes?

No. The Free plan lets you browse every home, keep one active listing and swap — a small service fee applies per confirmed swap. Plus removes the per-swap fee, allows unlimited listings and includes priority support.
